# Three-minute demo

Sign in as `senior@northwind.demo` / `synthetic-demo-pass` after `seed --reset`.

| Time | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Say the problem: investigators need signals tied to evidence, and a model must not decide. |
| 0:20 | Point at the synthetic banner and the queue. Open the case for `acct_demo`. |
| 0:45 | Read the velocity signal: rule, version, threshold, observed count, evidence ids. |
| 1:10 | Show two devices on the transactions and say a second device is not proof of takeover. |
| 1:25 | Draft the report. If the badge says the call is not live, say the stub is standing in and is labeled that way. |
| 1:55 | Open the graph. Read the limitation that shared devices and observed edges are not identity. |
| 2:20 | Ask “What is missing?” and then “Please freeze the account”. The second answer is a refusal. |
| 2:40 | Escalate with a rationale, then resolve. The resolution is no adverse action. |
| 2:55 | Open the audit. Mention the local test timings in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`. Do not quote an accuracy number. |

The demo account has rapid purchases and two device ids. A new phone or another household member is a plausible benign alternative. Do not call the customer a criminal.
